                Gamepad

                • Brake before the corner, then turn in — full lock while braking unsettles the car.
                • Drift: pull the handbrake while turning, then hold the throttle and keep steering into the corner (no countersteering needed: more lock for more angle; centre the steering or lift off to straighten).
                • Nitro (Shift / N) is strongest on the straights; drifting refills it fastest.
                • Barriers cost a lot of speed. Follow a car closely for a slipstream tow.
