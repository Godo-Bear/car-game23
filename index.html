      • Brake before the corner, then turn in — braking with full lock unsettles the car.
      • Drift: pull the handbrake (Space) while turning, then hold the throttle to keep sliding — the car countersteers for you, steer to adjust the angle. Lift off to straighten up.
      • Hitting the barriers costs a lot of speed. Follow a car closely for a slipstream tow.
